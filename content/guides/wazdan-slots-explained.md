**Wazdan** is a Polish-Maltese studio best known for its **Coins** games and for building nearly everything from the same small toolkit of features. Once you understand four of them – Hold the Jackpot, Chance Level, Cash Infinity and Cash Out – you can pick up almost any Wazdan slot in a few spins.

Browse all of them on our [Wazdan page](/providers/wazdan).

## Hold the Jackpot

Wazdan's take on [hold and win](/guides/hold-and-win-explained). Landing enough Bonus symbols starts a round of three respins; every new symbol sticks and resets the counter to three. Symbols carry cash values or jackpot labels, and filling the whole grid pays the **Grand Jackpot**.

The Grand varies a lot between games: 500x in [Bumba Meu Boi Coin](/slots/wazdan/bumba-meu-boi-coin), 2,500x in [25 Coins Grand Gold Edition](/slots/wazdan/25-coins-grand-gold-edition) and 5,000x in [16 Coins x5000](/slots/wazdan/16-coins-x5000). The "x5000" or "x3000" in a Coins game's name is the Grand Jackpot.

## Chance Level

Wazdan's **ante bet**. You multiply your stake – typically x2, x5 or x10 – and the game triggers bonuses or jackpot features more often. In [16 Coins x5000](/slots/wazdan/16-coins-x5000), Wazdan says it raises bonus chances without changing prize values.

What it means in practice: you're paying more per spin to see the bonus sooner. It doesn't make the game cheaper to play; it makes sessions more feature-heavy and burns through a balance faster. Compare other games with this option in our [ante bet slots list](/best/ante-bet-slots), and use the [slot cost calculator](/tools/slot-cost-calculator) to see what a higher stake costs per hour.

## Cash Infinity and "to Infinity" features

**Cash Infinity** symbols stick to the reels until the end of the *next* bonus round, so the grid fills up between bonuses and the next Hold the Jackpot starts stronger. Wazdan has spun off variations:

- **Sticky to Infinity** – Mystery and Jackpot Mystery symbols stay until the next bonus ends ([Cash Grotto](/slots/wazdan/cash-grotto)).
- **Collect to Infinity** – Collectors stay active until the next bonus ends ([25 Coins Grand Gold Edition](/slots/wazdan/25-coins-grand-gold-edition)).

## Cash Out and Collectors

Newer Wazdan games add a **collector** layer on top. **Cash Out** symbols gather the values below them and pay them out – in [16 Coins x5000](/slots/wazdan/16-coins-x5000) after a countdown, with a boost of up to 300%. In [Mighty Wild: Gorilla](/slots/wazdan/mighty-wild-gorilla) and [Fisherman's Luck](/slots/wazdan/fishermans-luck), Collector or Gainer symbols stay for up to 10 spins, collecting cash and jackpot values.
