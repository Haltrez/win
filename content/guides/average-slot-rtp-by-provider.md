**RTP (return to player)** is the share of all money wagered that a slot pays back over a very long run of spins. It is the closest thing a slot has to a price tag: a 96% game keeps 4% of stakes over time, while a 94% game keeps 6%. If RTP is new to you, read our [RTP explainer](/guides/rtp-explained) first.

We wanted to know whether some studios are consistently cheaper or more expensive to play. So we took every slot on Scatter Drop with an **official, provider-published RTP** (711 of our 791 games) and grouped them by studio.

## How we built this

- **Only official numbers.** Every RTP comes from the provider's own game page or game sheet. We left out slots where the provider doesn't publish an RTP (mostly older Hacksaw titles) and the two Hacksaw games where we only have third-party figures.
- **One number per game.** When a provider lists several RTP versions, we used the default or highest one, because that is the figure studios headline. We cover the lower versions further down.
- **Averages and medians.** The average is pulled down by a few low-RTP games, so the median (the middle value) is often the better guide to a "typical" game.

## What stands out

### BGaming publishes the most high-RTP games

BGaming has the highest median of the big five, and **97 of its 230 slots (42%) are listed at 97% or more**. Games like [Cat's Soup](/slots/bgaming/cats-soup) at 98.06% sit near the top of our [97%+ RTP list](/best/97-rtp-slots). BGaming also has the widest spread: 16 of its games are below 95%, down to 92.90% for the brand-new [Bling Blitz Power Nudge](/slots/bgaming/bling-blitz-power-nudge). Checking the individual game still matters.

### Pragmatic Play clusters tightly around 96.5%

Pragmatic Play's median is exactly **96.50%**, and 119 of its 195 games are at 96.5% or above. Only one game in our sample, Mighty Kong, is listed below 95%. Pragmatic has a few outliers at the top too: [The Catfather](/slots/pragmatic-play/the-catfather) is listed at 98.10%, and the new [Sweet Bonanza POP](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza-pop) at 97.05%.

### Nolimit City is the most consistent

Every one of Nolimit City's 130 slots sits between **95.91% and 96.77%**. If you play Nolimit, you can expect close to 96% on almost every title. What differs between their games is volatility, not price; see our guide to [Nolimit City's xMechanics](/guides/nolimit-city-x-mechanics-explained).

### Thunderkick has the biggest split

Thunderkick's median (96.10%) is normal, but its average is the lowest of the big five because **35 of its 107 games are published at about 94%**, for example [The Golden Pot and Pints](/slots/thunderkick/the-golden-pot-and-pints) at 94.08%. At the other end is [1429 Uncharted Seas](/slots/thunderkick/1429-uncharted-seas) at 98.60%, the highest RTP on our whole site. With Thunderkick, the specific game matters more than with any other studio we track.

### Hacksaw: high headline numbers, many versions

Hacksaw headlines around 96.3%, with [Le Prechaun](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-prechaun) at 97.52%. But **29 of the 31 Hacksaw games with an official RTP also list lower versions**, some as low as 86%. That makes Hacksaw the clearest example of the next point.

## The catch: one game, several RTPs

Many studios let casinos choose from several RTP settings for the same game. Pragmatic Play and Hacksaw Gaming publish these lists openly. Hacksaw's Le Bandit, for example, comes in 96.34%, 94.23%, 92.17% and 88.36% versions. The game looks identical in each; only the long-term return changes.

So a provider's average tells you what the studio *offers*, not what a given casino *runs*. To see the real figure:

1. Open the game and find the **info or paytable screen**.
2. Look for the line stating the theoretical RTP, usually near the bottom of the rules.
3. Compare it with the official figure on our slot page.

Our guide on [how to check a slot's RTP](/guides/how-to-check-slot-rtp) walks through this step by step. Our [variable-RTP slots list](/best/variable-rtp-slots) shows which games are known to come in more than one version.

## Does a 0.3% difference matter?

Over a single session, hardly at all. [Volatility](/guides/slot-volatility-explained) decides far more about how a short session feels than a few tenths of RTP. Over many sessions the gap adds up (our [slot cost calculator](/tools/slot-cost-calculator) shows it in money for any game), and the bigger gaps (96.5% vs 94%, or 96% vs 88%) matter even over a few hundred spins. As a rough guide:

- **96.5% or more:** above average for modern slots.
- **95.5–96.5%:** typical.
- **Below 95%:** on the expensive side. Check whether a higher version exists.

## Key takeaways

- The typical online slot returns **about 96.2%** according to official figures.
- **BGaming** and **Pragmatic Play** have the highest averages among large catalogues; **Nolimit City** is the most consistent.

- **RTP versions** matter more than the studio: always check the info screen.
