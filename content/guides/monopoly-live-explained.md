**Monopoly Live** is Evolution's game-show version of its Dream Catcher money wheel, built with Hasbro's Monopoly brand and launched in April 2019. A live host spins the wheel; when it lands on a bonus segment, the game switches to a 3D augmented-reality board where **Mr. Monopoly** walks around collecting multipliers.

There's **no free version** – it's a live broadcast. For free play, see our [slot demos](/slots).

## The wheel

The wheel has 54 segments and six bet spots:

| Bet spot | Pays |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1:1 |
| 2 | 2:1 |
| 5 | 5:1 |
| 10 | 10:1 |
| Chance | instant cash prize or a multiplier |
| 2 Rolls / 4 Rolls | the bonus board |

Pick your spots, the host spins, and the result pays or triggers a feature.

## Chance

If the wheel lands on **Chance**, Mr. Monopoly shows a card: either a cash prize paid immediately, or a **multiplier** that stays in play for the next spin. If the next spin lands on Chance again, the multipliers multiply together.

## The bonus board (2 Rolls and 4 Rolls)

This is why people play. When the wheel stops on **2 Rolls** or **4 Rolls**, everyone who bet on that segment goes to the board:

1. Before the rolls, random **houses and hotels** are placed on properties, boosting their multipliers.
2. Two dice are rolled the stated number of times. Mr. Monopoly moves around the board, and every property he lands on adds its multiplier to your total.
3. **Doubles** give an extra roll. Landing on **Go to Jail** needs doubles to escape; **Go** pays a bonus; Chance and Community Chest add surprises; Tax squares deduct.
4. At the end, all collected multipliers are added up and applied to your 2 Rolls / 4 Rolls bet.

4 Rolls is rarer than 2 Rolls (one segment vs three), which is why it can pay far more.

## RTP

Evolution's help screen lists the RTP per bet. Independent sources report **96.23% on number 1**, with lower figures on the other numbers and the bonus spots. Betting on 1 keeps your money in play longest; the bonus spots trade some return for the board round.

## How to approach it

A common approach is small bets on 2 Rolls and 4 Rolls every spin so you never miss a board round, with a number bet to cover the gaps. No bet selection changes the long-run RTP, so size your stakes for a session you'd be happy to lose.
