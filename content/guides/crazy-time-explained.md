**Crazy Time** is Evolution's flagship live game show: a giant 54-segment money wheel spun by a live host, with four bonus games built around it. Evolution launched it in July 2020 as the biggest game it had ever produced, and it's still the most-played live game show online.

There is **no free demo** – it's a live broadcast with real bets. If you want free play, every slot on Scatter Drop has a [free demo](/slots); the live game can only be watched or played for real money at a casino.

## The wheel and the bets

The wheel has **54 segments** across eight bet spots:

| Bet spot | Segments | Pays |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 21 | 1:1 |
| 2 | 13 | 2:1 |
| 5 | 7 | 5:1 |
| 10 | 4 | 10:1 |
| Coin Flip | 4 | bonus game |
| Cash Hunt | 2 | bonus game |
| Pachinko | 2 | bonus game |
| Crazy Time | 1 | bonus game |

You place bets on any spots you like, the host spins the wheel, and the flapper decides the result. Number bets pay their multiplier; bonus segments take everyone who bet on them into that bonus game.

## Top Slot

Before every spin, a **Top Slot** reel spins above the wheel and matches one bet spot with a multiplier (up to 50x). If the wheel then lands on that spot, the multiplier applies – to the number payout, or to the bonus game's prizes. That's where the big wins come from.

## The four bonus games

- **Coin Flip** – a coin with a red side and a blue side, each assigned a multiplier. The coin is flipped; you win the multiplier on the side that lands up. The most frequent bonus.
- **Cash Hunt** – a wall of 108 random multipliers is shown, then hidden behind symbols and shuffled. You pick one target; you win whatever multiplier sits behind it.
- **Pachinko** – a puck drops down a pegged wall and lands on a multiplier. "Double" slots double every multiplier on the wall and drop again.
- **Crazy Time** – the big one. A huge virtual wheel with three flappers (you pick a colour). Multipliers go far higher than the main wheel, and "Double" and "Triple" segments multiply the whole wheel and spin again.

## RTP and max win

Evolution lists the RTP per bet spot in the game's help screen. Independent sources report it as **96.08% on number 1**, slightly lower on 2, 5 and 10, and lowest on the bonus spots, down to about **94.3% on Pachinko**. In practice: number bets return the most over time; bonus bets pay for the chance of a big multiplier.

The **top multiplier is listed as 20,000x** your bet, in the Crazy Time bonus with a Top Slot multiplier applied. It's rare. Treat the bonus games as the entertainment, not the plan.

## Is it worth playing?

If you like slots for the bonus rounds, Crazy Time is that feeling with a host and a crowd. Bets on 1 keep you in the game longest; spreading small bets across the bonus spots gives you a shot at every bonus game. Decide your budget before you sit down, and keep in mind that the house edge on the bonus spots is higher than on most slots.
