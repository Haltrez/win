Players often pick slots by studio, because each studio has a recognisable style. Here is how the five biggest catalogues on Scatter Drop compare, using only the data the providers publish. Averages are based on the games we list and update as our catalogue grows.

| Provider | Slots listed | Avg. published RTP | Median max win | Most common volatility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [BGaming](/providers/bgaming) | 227 | ~96.5% | 5,000x | Very high |
| [Pragmatic Play](/providers/pragmatic-play) | 185 | ~96.4% | ~5,300x | High (where published) |
| [Nolimit City](/providers/nolimit-city) | 130 | ~96.1% | 20,000x | Extreme / Insane |
| [Thunderkick](/providers/thunderkick) | 107 | ~95.6% | ~8,300x | High |
| [Hacksaw Gaming](/providers/hacksaw-gaming) | 102 | ~96.3%* | 10,000x | Mixed |

*Hacksaw publishes RTP for only some of its games; the average uses those figures.

## Pragmatic Play – high-volume hit makers

Pragmatic releases new games almost every week and owns some of the most-played titles in the world, including [Gates of Olympus](/slots/pragmatic-play/gates-of-olympus) and [Sweet Bonanza](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza). Expect polished scatter-pays and tumble games, frequent sequels ("1000" editions) and bonus buys.

## BGaming – data-rich and varied

BGaming publishes unusually detailed game sheets – RTP, volatility, hit rate and bonus frequency – which is why we can show so much data for its games. The catalogue mixes crypto-flavoured classics, fruit games and high-volatility features; [Mystic Reels](/slots/bgaming/mystic-reels) tops its max-win list in our database.

## Nolimit City – maximum volatility

Nolimit City is known for dark themes, patented [xMechanics](/guides/nolimit-city-x-mechanics-explained) and enormous max wins: [Tombstone Slaughter](/slots/nolimit-city/tombstone-slaughter) advertises 500,000x. Most of its modern games are rated Extreme or Insane volatility – fun in demo mode, demanding with real money.

## Thunderkick – art and personality

Thunderkick is the most art-driven studio here, from [Esqueleto Explosivo](/slots/thunderkick/esqueleto-explosivo) to [1429 Uncharted Seas](/slots/thunderkick/1429-uncharted-seas), whose 98.6% RTP is one of the highest published anywhere. Note that several recent Thunderkick releases publish RTPs around 94%, which pulls the studio average down.

## Hacksaw Gaming – tiered bonuses and DuelReels

Hacksaw built its reputation on compact grids, three-tier bonuses and the [DuelReels](/guides/duelreels-explained) mechanic. Its "Le" series starring Smokey the raccoon – from [Le Bandit](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-bandit) to [Le Cowboy](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-cowboy) – is one of the most recognisable in slots.

## How to choose

Studio style is a useful shortcut, but the numbers on each game matter more. Compare RTP, volatility and max win on every slot page, and browse by what you want rather than who made it – for example [high RTP slots](/best/high-rtp-slots) or [low volatility slots](/best/low-volatility-slots).
