"Jackpot" on a slot can mean very different things. On most modern games it's a fixed prize, a set multiple of your bet, won inside a bonus round. On a small number of games it's a progressive pool that grows with every bet. We have **121 slots with jackpots** on our site, and almost all of them are the fixed kind.

## Fixed jackpots: a multiple of your bet

A fixed jackpot is written into the game's paytable as a multiplier. If the Grand is 1,000x and you bet €0.20, it pays €200. Bet €1 and it pays €1,000. The odds of hitting it don't change with your stake; only the size does.

Most games stack several tiers. Some real examples from official data:

| Slot | Jackpot tiers |
|---|---|
| [Blazing Fire Pots](/slots/bgaming/blazing-fire-pots-hold-and-spin) | Mini 25x, Minor 50x, Major 100x, Mega 1,000x, Grand 5,000x |
| [Aloha King Elvis](/slots/bgaming/aloha-king-elvis) | Mini 20x, Major 100x, Mega 1,000x |
| [Monster Hunt](/slots/bgaming/monster-hunt) | Mini 20x, Mega 100x, Grand 1,000x |
| [Mustang Gold Megaways](/slots/pragmatic-play/mustang-gold-megaways) | Four jackpots from 20x to 2,500x |
| [16 Coins x5000](/slots/wazdan/16-coins-x5000) | Grand Jackpot of 5,000x |

Notice that the same name can mean different values. A "Mega" is the top prize in one game and second-from-top in another, so always check the paytable.

## How jackpots are usually won

Almost all of these games hide the jackpots inside a [hold and win](/guides/hold-and-win-explained) style bonus. Coins land, stick to the grid, and reset your respins. Jackpot symbols can appear among the coins, and in many games **filling the whole grid** pays the top prize. Of the 121 jackpot slots on our site, **76 use a hold-and-win round**.

Other routes you'll see:

- **Filling rows:** in Monster Hunt and Aloha King Elvis, each completed row of coins unlocks the next jackpot tier.
- **Scatter counts:** in Wazdan's Mighty Crown games, 2, 3, 4 or 5 Crown Scatters award different jackpots. [Mighty Crown: Legacy of Mars](/slots/wazdan/mighty-crown-legacy-of-mars) also pays its 3,000x Grand for a full screen of Sevens.
- **Wheels and picks:** some games use a jackpot wheel or a pick-and-match bonus instead of a respin grid.

## Progressive jackpots

A progressive jackpot works differently. A small part of every bet goes into a shared pool, which keeps growing until someone wins it, then resets to a starting amount. Some pools are linked across many casinos, which is how they can grow so large.

Two things are worth knowing:

1. **The odds are tiny.** A pool can only grow so big because almost nobody wins it.
2. **The base RTP is lower.** The slice that funds the pool comes out of every spin, so the rest of the game usually pays back less.

Progressive jackpot games aren't part of our current database, which focuses on games with published fixed RTP, volatility and max win data.

## Jackpot vs max win

A jackpot is one specific prize. The **max win** is the cap on everything a single round can pay, including jackpots plus any other wins. In many hold-and-win games the Grand Jackpot and the max win are the same number. In others the max win is higher, because coin values and multipliers can add to the jackpot. See [how max win works](/guides/how-max-win-works).

## Are jackpot slots worth playing?

They suit players who enjoy the build-up of a respin round more than ordinary line wins. Just remember that fixed jackpots are part of the game's normal RTP: the top tiers are rare by design, and most bonus rounds end with a handful of coins.
