An **ante bet** is an optional switch that makes every spin cost a bit more in exchange for a better chance of triggering the bonus. Different studios give it different names: **Ante Bet** at Pragmatic Play, **Chance x2** or **Respin Ante** at BGaming, **Chance Level** at Wazdan, and **xBet** at Nolimit City. We've tagged **158 slots** on our site with a feature like this.

## What it costs and what you get

The deal varies by game. Some real examples from official game data:

| [Backyard Grill](/slots/bgaming/backyard-grill) | +25% per spin | Chance x2: scatters land more often |

Wazdan's version is the most extreme. Chance Level can multiply your stake several times over, with settings such as x2, x4 and x6 in [20 Coins](/slots/wazdan/20-coins). Read more in our [Wazdan guide](/guides/wazdan-slots-explained).

## A simple way to think about it

Take Gates of Olympus. With the ante bet, each spin costs 1.25x and scatters are twice as likely. If the bonus also becomes about twice as frequent, you'd wager roughly **1.25 ÷ 2 = 0.625 times** as much per bonus as you would without it. In other words, bonuses come around more quickly for your money.

But that doesn't mean you win more. The game is balanced so the long-term return stays about the same, which means more of the RTP sits in the bonus and less in ordinary spins. You trade a steadier base game for more frequent bonus moments.

## Ante bet vs bonus buy

Both shift you towards the bonus, but in different ways:

- **Bonus buy:** one big payment, instant bonus, no base game. The fastest and swingiest option.
- **Ante bet:** a small extra on every spin, more frequent bonuses, base game still in play. Smaller swings than buying, but each spin costs more.
- **Neither:** normal spins and the natural trigger rate. Slowest, steadiest.

Some games won't let you combine them. In Big Atlantis Frenzy, for example, you can buy free spins or buy Chance x3, but not both. Our [bonus buy vs natural bonus guide](/guides/bonus-buy-vs-natural-bonus) compares the buy and natural routes with real trigger rates.

## The catch: your budget runs out faster

The biggest effect of an ante bet is simple: every spin costs more. At a €1 stake, a 25% ante makes each spin €1.25, so a €100 budget lasts 80 spins instead of 100. With Wazdan's higher Chance Level settings, the same budget can last a fraction as long.

If you turn it on, lower your base stake to keep the real cost per spin where you want it. Our [bankroll management guide](/guides/bankroll-management-for-slots) shows how to size sessions.
