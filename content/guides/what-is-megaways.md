**Megaways** is a reel mechanic created by Big Time Gaming (BTG) and first used in Bonanza (2016). Instead of a fixed grid, each reel shows a random number of symbols (typically 2–7) on every spin. The number of ways to win is the product of the symbols on each reel – up to **117,649** on a standard six-reel layout.

## How ways are calculated

If six reels show 7, 7, 7, 7, 7 and 7 symbols, there are 7⁶ = 117,649 ways. If they show 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7, there are 5,040. Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of row.

## Common companions

Most Megaways slots combine the mechanic with **cascades** (winning symbols are replaced) and an **unlimited win multiplier** in free spins that grows with every cascade.

## Licensing

BTG licenses Megaways to other studios, which is why you'll find Megaways games from Pragmatic Play, Red Tiger, Blueprint and many more.
