**Megaways** is a reel mechanic created by Big Time Gaming (BTG) and first used in Bonanza (2016). Instead of a fixed grid, each reel shows a random number of symbols (typically 2–7) on every spin. The number of ways to win is the product of the symbols on each reel – up to **117,649** on a standard six-reel layout.

## How ways are calculated

If six reels show 7, 7, 7, 7, 7 and 7 symbols, there are 7⁶ = 117,649 ways. If they show 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7, there are 5,040. Wins pay for matching symbols on adjacent reels from left to right, regardless of row.

## Common companions

Most Megaways slots combine the mechanic with **cascades** (winning symbols are replaced) and an **unlimited win multiplier** in free spins that grows with every cascade.

## Licensing

BTG licenses Megaways to other studios, which is why you'll find Megaways games from Pragmatic Play, Red Tiger, Blueprint and many more.

## How the ways are calculated

On a Megaways slot, each reel shows a random number of symbols – often 2 to 7 – on every spin, and some games add a horizontal reel on top. The number of ways is simply the reel heights multiplied together. Six reels of seven symbols give 7 x 7 x 7 x 7 x 7 x 7 = **117,649 ways**. A spin with short reels might only give a few hundred.

## Megaways and its relatives

Big Time Gaming invented and licensed the format, and many studios now release Megaways versions of existing hits, such as [The Dog House Megaways](/slots/pragmatic-play/the-dog-house-megaways) and [Extra Juicy Megaways](/slots/pragmatic-play/extra-juicy-megaways). Other studios built their own variable-ways systems:

- **Nolimit City xWays** – mystery symbols that make a reel taller only on the spin they land, as in [The Crypt](/slots/nolimit-city/the-crypt). See [xMechanics explained](/guides/nolimit-city-x-mechanics-explained).
- **Thunderkick random reel heights** – [Carnival Queen 2](/slots/thunderkick/carnival-queen-2) and [Eager Beavers](/slots/thunderkick/eager-beavers) change reel heights each spin for up to 117,649 or even 1,000,000 ways.

## Cascades and unlimited multipliers

Most Megaways games add tumbling reels, so a win removes symbols and new ones drop in – read [tumbling reels explained](/guides/tumbling-reels-explained). Many also have a free spins multiplier that rises by +1 with every cascade and doesn't reset, which is where the large max wins come from.

## Should you play Megaways?

Megaways games feel busy: lots of small combinations, with the real value in long cascade chains during free spins. Most are high volatility.
