**Lightning Roulette** is European roulette with a twist: before each spin, one to five **Lucky Numbers** are struck by lightning and given random multipliers from **50x to 500x**. If the ball lands on a Lucky Number you bet on straight-up, you win the multiplier instead of the normal payout. Evolution launched it in 2018, and it has won multiple industry "game of the year" awards.

It's a live table with a real dealer and **no free demo**. The RNG version, *First Person Lightning Roulette*, sometimes has a demo at casinos. For free slot play, see our [slot demos](/slots).

## How a round works

1. Place bets as in normal roulette: straight-up numbers, splits, outside bets (red/black, odd/even, dozens and so on).
2. The lightning strikes: **1 to 5 numbers** light up with multipliers between 50x and 500x.
3. The dealer spins the wheel.
4. If the ball lands on a Lucky Number that you bet on **straight-up**, you win the multiplier. Any other straight-up win pays **29:1**. Outside bets pay their normal odds.

The reduced 29:1 payout is how the multipliers are funded. Over many spins, the lower base payout and the occasional multiplier balance out to the RTP below.

## RTP

Independent sources report **97.30%** for Lightning Roulette, the same as standard European roulette, for the outside bets. **Straight-up bets are reported slightly lower, around 97.1%**, which is the cost of the multiplier chance. Either way, it's a higher return than almost any slot, where 96% is typical. Check the figure in the game's help screen at your casino.

## Strategy that is actually just maths

- **Only straight-up bets can win a multiplier.** Splits and outside bets are normal roulette.
- Covering many numbers straight-up raises the chance of hitting a Lucky Number but costs more per spin; the expected return doesn't change.
- A single 500x hit is rare. Most multipliers are 50x or 100x.

No betting pattern beats the house edge; the multipliers make it more fun, not more profitable.
