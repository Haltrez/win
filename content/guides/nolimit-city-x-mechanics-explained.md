Nolimit City builds most of its slots from a toolkit of patented mechanics it calls **xMechanics**. Once you know what each one does, a Nolimit game that looks chaotic becomes much easier to read – and it becomes obvious why the studio's slots carry some of the highest max wins in the industry.

Nolimit publishes volatility as a number rather than a label. On its own website the numbers are grouped into **Medium** (below 9.9), **High** (9.9–17.3), **Extreme** (17.3–27.3) and **Insane** (27.3 and above). Most games built around stacked xMechanics land in Extreme or Insane. See all of them in our [xWays, xNudge & xBomb slots list](/best/nolimit-x-mechanics-slots).

## xNudge: wilds that grow as they move

An **xNudge Wild** is a tall, stacked wild. When only part of it lands on the reel, it nudges one row at a time until it is fully visible, and **every nudge adds +1 to its multiplier**. A three-row wild that lands with only its top showing nudges twice and finishes as a x3 wild.

In [The Crypt](/slots/nolimit-city/the-crypt) the twist is reversed: the wild drops to the bottom row, gaining a step multiplier on the way, then expands to cover the reel. In [Tombstone R.I.P](/slots/nolimit-city/tombstone-rip) several xNudge wilds can line up across the middle reels, and their multipliers combine – one reason that game advertises a 300,000x ceiling.

**What to watch for:** wilds that land almost entirely off-screen. They look disappointing but nudge the furthest.

## xWays: one position, many symbols

An **xWays** symbol is a mystery block. When it reveals, it shows two, three or more of the same symbol stacked inside one position. Because the reel now holds more symbols, the number of ways to win jumps for that spin.

Two features make xWays stronger:

- **All xWays on the grid reveal the same symbol**, so three of them across adjacent reels can produce a large ways win on their own.
- **Infectious xWays** spread that revealed symbol to other positions, as in [Das xBoot](/slots/nolimit-city/das-xboot) and many later releases.

[San Quentin xWays](/slots/nolimit-city/san-quentin-xways) is the classic example: Nolimit rates it High rather than Extreme volatility, but stacked xWays reels are what let it reach a 150,000x cap.

## xBomb: clearing space and raising the multiplier

An **xBomb Wild** substitutes like a normal wild and then explodes, removing neighbouring symbols so new ones can drop in. Each explosion adds +1 to a running win multiplier for that round.

xBomb fits naturally with cascading or "collapsing" grids. In [Fire in the Hole xBomb](/slots/nolimit-city/fire-in-the-hole-xbomb) the mine starts with three active rows and opens a new row each time symbols collapse, so a long xBomb chain both grows the grid and lifts the multiplier. [Dead Canary](/slots/nolimit-city/dead-canary) uses a cross-shaped blast instead.

## xSplit: doubling symbols in place

**xSplit** symbols split the symbols on their row or reel in half, turning one symbol into two and often doubling a multiplier at the same time. Because splits multiply the symbol count, they combine well with ways-based grids. Look for xSplit wilds that land on the last reel: they split everything to their left.

## xBet and boosters: paying for volatility

**xBet** (and similar "booster" options) lets you pay more per spin – often 2x to 3x the base bet – to make bonus triggers more likely. It changes how often bonuses appear, not your long-term expected return. Treat it as a higher stake, never as a shortcut, and remember that some regulated markets disable feature buys and boosters altogether.

## How the mechanics stack

The reason Nolimit slots swing so hard is that the mechanics **multiply each other**. In [Mental](/slots/nolimit-city/mental), xWays can sit under xSplit and xNudge effects at the same time, so a single spin can combine a taller reel, split symbols and a nudged multiplier. Most spins land none of them; very occasionally all of them line up.
