A **social casino** and a **sweepstakes casino** often look identical – same lobby, same slots, same coin packages. The difference is whether there's a prize component.

## Side by side

| | Social casino | Sweepstakes casino |
|---|---|---|
| Coins | Gold Coins only | Gold Coins **and** Sweeps Coins |
| Can you redeem prizes? | No | Yes – Sweeps Coins only |
| Purchase necessary? | Never | Never |
| Where available | Almost all US states | Most states, but not all ([see list](/guides/sweepstakes-casino-states)) |
| ID check | Usually not needed | Before your first prize redemption |

## Social casino: pure entertainment

In a social casino you play slot-style games with **Gold Coins** that have no real-world value. You get them free and can buy more packages if you want to keep playing. There's nothing to redeem – it's entertainment, like a mobile game.

## Sweepstakes casino: free play with prizes

A sweepstakes casino adds **Sweeps Coins**, which you can't buy – they come free as gifts, from daily rewards, by mail-in request or as a free extra with Gold Coin packages. Sweeps Coins won in play can be redeemed for prizes after an identity check. That's what makes it a sweepstakes: no purchase is necessary to enter or to win. [How sweepstakes casinos work](/guides/how-sweepstakes-casinos-work) explains it step by step.

## Same site, two versions

Many brands run both. In states that restrict sweepstakes play – California and New York, for example – the same site offers only the Gold Coin social version. Everywhere it's allowed, you get the full sweepstakes version.
