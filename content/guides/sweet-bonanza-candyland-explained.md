**Sweet Bonanza CandyLand** is Pragmatic Play's live game show built on its hit slot [Sweet Bonanza](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza). A live host spins a candy-coloured wheel with three bonus game areas around it, and the main bonus is **Sweet Spins** – free spins of a live version of the slot itself.

It's a 24/7 live broadcast with **no demo**. The original Sweet Bonanza slot, and its sequel [Sweet Bonanza 1000](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza-1000), both have free demos here.

## The wheel and the bets

Six betting positions: the numbers **1, 2 and 5**, and three bonus games – **Sweet Spins**, **Candy Drop** and **Bubble Surprise**. Between them sit three **Sugar Bomb** segments.

## Sugar Bombs

Land on a Sugar Bomb and the wheel awards a **random multiplier of up to 10x** plus a **free re-spin**. If you've selected the optional **Sugar Bomb Booster** (a side bet), the multiplier is doubled. Land on another Sugar Bomb on the re-spin and the multipliers multiply together before the next result.

## The three bonus games

- **Sweet Spins** – 10 free spins of a live Sweet Bonanza slot. Wins come from collecting **eight or more matching symbols** anywhere on the grid, as in the original slot, and the more you collect, the more you win.
- **Candy Drop** – pick one of three candy balls. It drops through a virtual plinko wall, collecting multipliers on the way. Hit all four **Jackpot Boxes** and it pays the **1,000x max win** for this bonus.
- **Bubble Surprise** – a fast bonus that awards a 5x, 10x or 25x multiplier, or an entry into one of the other bonus games.

## Max win and RTP

Pragmatic Play states a **€500,000 maximum win** for the game. The RTP is shown in the game's help screen and differs by bet spot, as on other live wheels; check it at your casino before playing.

## Who it suits

Sweet Bonanza fans who want the tumbling, symbol-collecting feel with a live host. If you've never played the slot, try the [free Sweet Bonanza demo](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza) first – Sweet Spins plays the same way.
