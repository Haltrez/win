**Free spins** means two different things. Inside a slot, it's the **bonus round**: a set of spins that cost nothing and usually come with upgrades such as multipliers or expanding symbols. At a casino, it's a **promotion**: a number of spins handed out as a bonus, with its own rules. This guide covers both – first the bonus round, then how free spins promotions are structured.

**Quick answer – how do you trigger free spins?** In most slots you land **three or more scatter symbols** in one spin. The game's paytable lists the exact rule. You can't influence the trigger: every spin is random, so the only way to enter the bonus on demand is a bonus buy, where it's offered.

## How to trigger free spins and bonus rounds

The usual trigger is **three or more scatter symbols** in one spin. Variations include scatters that only land on certain reels, bonus symbols that must land on the same row, or meters that fill over several spins. Some games award extra spins for each additional scatter. Pragmatic's Super Scatter editions go further and pay an instant prize for special scatters in the trigger; see [Super Scatter slots explained](/guides/super-scatter-slots-explained).

## What makes free spins better than base spins

A bonus round is only exciting if something is improved compared to the base game. Common upgrades:

- **Expanding symbols** – a randomly chosen symbol expands to fill reels, as in [Book of Dead](/slots/play-n-go/book-of-dead) and other [book slots](/best/book-slots).
- **Sticky or growing multipliers** – multipliers that persist and climb through the round.
- **More special symbols** – higher odds of wilds, collectors or modifiers. [Big Bass Bonanza](/slots/pragmatic-play/big-bass-bonanza) adds fisherman collectors that only appear in the bonus.
- **Assured features** – a modifier that is triggered on every bonus spin.

## Tiered bonuses

Hacksaw Gaming popularised the **three-tier bonus**: three scatters give the basic round, four give an upgraded version, and a special symbol unlocks the top tier. In [Wanted Dead or a Wild](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/wanted-dead-or-a-wild) and [Chaos Crew](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/chaos-crew), the upper tiers are rare but carry most of the game's max-win potential. Nolimit City uses the same idea – [The Crypt](/slots/nolimit-city/the-crypt) has a standard and a more volatile bonus.

## Retriggers

A **retrigger** awards more spins when scatters land during the bonus. Generous retriggers can turn a short round into a long one; some games cap the total number of spins.

## Bonus buys

Many slots let you **buy** direct entry to the bonus for a fixed multiple of your stake, often 100x. A bonus buy does not improve your long-term return – it simply swaps many base-game spins for one expensive shortcut. Bonus buys are banned in Great Britain and some other markets. See [bonus buy explained](/guides/bonus-buy-explained).

## Free spins promotions: how the offers are structured

Casino free spins offers look simple, but most share the same building blocks. Check these in the terms before you take one:

- **Number of spins and spin value.** For example 50 spins at a fixed stake per spin. The stake is set by the casino, not by you.
- **Eligible games.** Spins are usually locked to one or a few named slots.
- **How winnings are paid.** Often as bonus money rather than cash, so it has to be played through before you can withdraw.
- **Wagering requirement.** How many times the winnings must be bet, such as 40x. Some offers are wager-free.
- **Maximum cashout.** A cap on what you can withdraw from the spins.
- **Expiry.** Spins and the bonus money usually expire after a set number of days.
- **Deposit or no deposit.** Welcome offers often tie spins to a first deposit; no-deposit spins usually have stricter caps.

The rules differ between casinos and countries, so the casino's own terms are the only reliable source. Taking a free spins offer is still real-money gambling – only play if you're 18+ and it's legal where you live.

## Free spins in social casino and demo games

The bonus round works the same in a **social casino or demo version**: the same scatters, the same trigger rules, the same features. The only difference is that you play with virtual coins. That makes demo mode a good way to see how often a game's free spins really trigger before you ever consider playing it for money – every slot on Scatter Drop has a free demo.
