**Super Scatter** is a mechanic Pragmatic Play added to new editions of its biggest hits: [Gates of Olympus](/slots/pragmatic-play/gates-of-olympus-super-scatter), [Sweet Bonanza](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza-super-scatter), [Sugar Rush](/slots/pragmatic-play/sugar-rush-super-scatter), [Starlight Princess](/slots/pragmatic-play/starlight-princess-super-scatter) and [Mahjong Wins](/slots/pragmatic-play/mahjong-wins-super-scatter). The base games barely change. What's new is a special scatter that pays an instant prize when it helps trigger the bonus, and it lifts the max win from 5,000x–25,000x to 50,000x or more.

## How a Super Scatter works

1. **It counts as a scatter.** A Super Scatter helps trigger the free spins, just like a regular scatter.
2. **It adds an instant prize.** If any Super Scatters are part of the trigger, you win a cash prize before the free spins begin. The prize depends on how many there are.
3. **The free spins then play as normal**, with the usual multipliers of each game.

## What one, two, three or four pay

| Super Scatters in the trigger | Gates of Olympus / Sweet Bonanza / Starlight Princess | Mahjong Wins |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 100x | 50x |
| 2 | 500x | 500x |
| 3 | 5,000x | 5,000x |
| 4 | 50,000x | 100,000x |

Figures are from Pragmatic Play's official game pages. In Sugar Rush Super Scatter, Pragmatic says wins can reach 50,000x when at least one Super Scatter helps trigger the bonus.

## Super Scatter vs the originals

The originals cap out lower. [Gates of Olympus](/slots/pragmatic-play/gates-of-olympus) pays up to 5,000x and [Sweet Bonanza](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza) up to 21,175x. The Super Scatter editions keep the same feel and add a tiny chance of a far bigger result. Because RTP doesn't change much, that extra ceiling has to be paid for somewhere: it typically makes ordinary bonuses a little less generous. See our study on [max-win inflation](/guides/slot-max-win-inflation) for how this trend plays out across the market.

## How likely is a big Super Scatter hit?

Pragmatic doesn't publish the odds of landing two, three or four Super Scatters. They are designed to be rare, especially four, which is where the 50,000x lives. Treat it like a lottery ticket attached to an ordinary bonus, not a realistic target.
