**Funky Time** is Evolution's 1970s disco-themed game show, released in 2023 as a follow-up to Crazy Time. It uses a bigger **64-segment wheel**, replaces the numbers with the letters **P, L, A, Y**, and adds four bonus games.

It's a live broadcast with real bets and **no demo**. For free play, see our [slot demos](/slots).

## The wheel and the bets

Nine bet spots:

| Bet spot | What it is |
|---|---|
| 1 | the only number; pays 1:1 |
| P, L, A, Y | letter segments; each pays 25:1 |
| Bar | bonus game |
| Stayin' Alive | bonus game |
| Disco | bonus game |
| VIP Disco | bonus game (one segment, the rarest) |

## Digit multipliers

Before each spin, a **Digit** reel above the wheel assigns a random multiplier to one or more spots. If the wheel lands on a boosted spot, the multiplier applies to the payout or to the bonus game. It's the same idea as Crazy Time's Top Slot and it's where the large wins come from.

## The four bonus games

- **Bar** – three symbols (bar, 7, grapes) each get a multiplier; a cocktail shaker selects one. If a **Digit** multiplier was on Bar, it multiplies the result.
- **Stayin' Alive** – you pick a colour and get three lives. Balls are drawn; your colour adds multipliers, the other colour costs lives. The round ends when lives run out. Gold balls boost the multipliers.
- **Disco** – Mr. Funky dances across a lit floor; each tile he reaches adds a multiplier, and the floor keeps growing until the song ends.
- **VIP Disco** – the premium version: higher multipliers, a longer dance and the top potential in the game.

## RTP

Evolution lists the RTP per bet spot in the game. Independent sources report an **overall figure of about 95.99%**, with the letters and bonus spots slightly below the number 1 bet. Like Crazy Time, bets on 1 keep you playing longest; the bonus spots are the entertainment.

## Funky Time vs Crazy Time

More segments, more bonus games and a slightly different pace: Funky Time rounds take a little longer because of the dance-floor bonuses. If you've played Crazy Time, you'll find it familiar; if you haven't, [Crazy Time explained](/guides/crazy-time-explained) is the simpler starting point.
