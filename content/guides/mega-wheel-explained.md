**Mega Wheel** is Pragmatic Play's original live game show and its first live casino game: a 54-segment money wheel with nine numbers and a random **Mega Multiplier** every round. It's streamed 24/7 in HD, and there's a Spanish-language version, *Mega Wheel Latino*, hosted from a studio in Colombia.

Live only, **no demo**. For free play, see our [slot demos](/slots).

## The wheel

54 segments across nine numbers: **1, 2, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20, 30 and 40**. Bet on one or more numbers, or cover all nine in one tap. If the wheel stops on a number you bet on, you win your bet back plus that number's multiplier (a 1 pays 1:1, a 40 pays 40:1).

## Mega Multipliers

Before the wheel stops, one number is chosen at random as the **Mega Lucky Number** and assigned a random **Mega Multiplier**. If the wheel lands on that number and you bet on it, your payout is multiplied – **up to 500x your bet**, per Pragmatic Play. The Mega Lucky Number and its multiplier are shown on screen while the wheel is still spinning, which is where the suspense comes from.

## RTP

Pragmatic Play publishes an overall **RTP of 96.51%** for Mega Wheel. It doesn't publish per-number figures, because they depend on how often the Mega Multipliers land. As on the Evolution wheels, low numbers hit often and pay little; high numbers hit rarely and pay a lot, and the long-run return is roughly the same across them.

## Mega Wheel vs Dream Catcher

Both are plain money wheels. Dream Catcher uses 2x and 7x re-spin multipliers; Mega Wheel uses a single Mega Lucky Number with a bigger multiplier each round, so wins are rarer but larger.
