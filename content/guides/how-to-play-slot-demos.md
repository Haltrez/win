Every slot on Scatter Drop links to the **official demo** from its provider. Demos use play-money credits, need no sign-up and run the same rules as the real game – which makes them the best way to learn a slot before deciding whether it suits you.

## How to start a demo

1. Open any slot page and press **Play demo**. We load the provider's demo only when you click, so pages stay fast.
2. Use **Full screen** on mobile for the best layout.
3. Check the game's **info or paytable screen** – it shows the rules, features and usually the RTP of the version you are playing.

## What a demo is good for

- **Learning features.** See how often wilds, modifiers and bonus triggers actually appear.
- **Feeling the volatility.** Play a few hundred spins: do wins arrive steadily, or in rare bursts? Compare with the provider's [volatility rating](/guides/slot-volatility-explained).
- **Comparing similar games.** Try two slots back to back – our [comparison pages](/compare) list the numbers side by side.
- **Checking mobile layout and sound** before you ever spend money.

## What a demo can't tell you

- **Your real-money results.** Slot outcomes are random. A great demo session means nothing for the next session, demo or real.
- **The exact RTP at a casino.** Many games have several RTP versions, and casinos choose which one to run. The demo may use a different version. Learn [how to check a slot's RTP](/guides/how-to-check-slot-rtp).
- **How long a bonus takes to trigger "on average".** Even 1,000 demo spins is a small sample for a high-volatility slot.

## Good habits

- Set a time limit for demo play too – it is still gambling-style entertainment.
- Never chase a demo "pattern" with real money; there are no patterns.
- If you are under 18, or gambling is a problem for you, please don't play – see our [responsible gambling page](/responsible-gambling) for support.
