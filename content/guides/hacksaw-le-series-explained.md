The **Le series** is Hacksaw Gaming's most recognisable run of slots. Each game stars **Smokey**, a raccoon who reappears as a bandit, a pharaoh, a Viking, a vampire, a football fan and more. The theme changes every time, but most games share a toolkit of coins, clovers and rainbow symbols, which makes the series easy to pick up once you know one game.

Browse every game in our [Hacksaw Le series list](/best/hacksaw-le-series-slots), each with a free official demo.

## The shared mechanics

Most Le games combine three ideas:

1. **Marked or Golden Squares.** In cluster games like [Le Bandit](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-bandit) and [Le King](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-king), the positions behind winning symbols are marked. They stay marked as the reels cascade.
2. **A Rainbow trigger.** When a Rainbow (or Neon Rainbow) symbol lands, marked squares are activated and reveal **Coins** (instant cash prizes), **Clovers** (which multiply coin values) and collectors such as Buckets of Gold or Treasure Pots.
3. **Three bonus games.** Landing three, four or five FS symbols usually triggers one of three escalating bonuses, where marked squares persist or stronger symbols appear more often.

The paylines games, such as [Le Pharaoh](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-pharaoh), [Le Sortudo](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-sortudo) and [Le Bandit Hold & Win](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-bandit-hold-and-win), swap the cluster system for coins, jackpot markers and Hold & Win respins.

## How the games differ

- **Biggest ceiling:** [Le Cowboy](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-cowboy) at 25,000x, then [Le Zeus](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-zeus) and [Le Santa](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-santa) at 20,000x.
- **Gentlest:** [Le Football Fan](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-football-fan), rated 2/5, with a 2,500x cap.
- **Best published RTP:** [Le Prechaun](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-prechaun) and [Le Vampire](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-vampire), with top versions of 97.52% and 97%.
- **Jackpot-focused:** [Le King](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-king) adds four jackpots through Jackpot Markers; [Le Sortudo](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-sortudo) and [Le Bandit Hold & Win](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-bandit-hold-and-win) use two Hold & Win games.
- **Something different:** [Le Digger](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-digger) digs through three layers of the grid with dynamite before reaching a golden layer of coins.

## RTP versions: check before you play

Almost every Le game is offered to casinos in several RTP versions. Le Bandit, for example, comes at 96.34%, 94.23%, 92.17% and 88.36%. The game looks identical in each version, so open the info screen to see which one you're playing.
