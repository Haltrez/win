**Hold and win** – also called *cash collect respins*, *hold and spin* or *link* features – is one of the most popular bonus formats in online slots. It is easy to follow on screen and builds tension one respin at a time.

## How the feature works

1. **Trigger:** land a set number of coin or bonus symbols – often six – in one spin.
2. **Lock:** those symbols stick to the grid. Everything else is cleared.
3. **Respins:** you get a counter, usually three spins. Only coins, special symbols and blanks can land.
4. **Reset:** each new coin sticks and **resets the counter to three**.
5. **Payout:** when the counter runs out, or the grid is full, all coin values are added together and paid.

## What decides how much you win

- **Coin values.** Coins usually carry a multiple of your bet. In [Le Viking](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-viking), bronze, silver and gold coins and diamonds reveal values at the end, starting as low as 0.2x.
- **Modifiers.** Collector symbols gather the value of every coin on the grid; multipliers boost them. In [Stormborn](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/stormborn), Collector Chests scoop up every coin and can apply a multiplier first.
- **Jackpots.** Fixed jackpot symbols add tiered prizes. [The Jade Path Hold & Win](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/the-jade-path-hold-and-win) has five jackpot tiers, and its Jaguar Multipliers boost every coin on the board.
- **Persistent payers.** In [Money Train 3](/slots/relax-gaming/money-train-3), special symbols keep acting on every respin, which is how that game reaches a 100,000x cap.

## Hold and win vs. free spins

A free spins round plays out like normal spins with improvements. A hold and win round only cares about coins and modifiers: most spins either add a coin or do nothing. That makes the bonus easier to read, but it also means the payout depends heavily on how many respins you survive.

## Tips

- Count how many coins it takes to trigger – six is common, fewer means more frequent bonuses with smaller grids.
- Check whether collectors and multipliers can appear in the base game or only in the respins.
- As always, play the demo first: hold and win rounds are easy to judge after a dozen triggers.
