Wilds substitute for other symbols. **Sticky wilds** go one step further: they stay put for more than one spin, so the same wild can help form wins again and again. Pair them with **respins** and you get some of the most readable features in slots.

## Types of sticky wild

- **Sticky for the bonus.** The simplest form: any wild that lands in free spins stays until the round ends. [The Dog House](/slots/pragmatic-play/the-dog-house) is the classic example, with multiplier wilds that stay for the whole bonus.
- **Sticky until used.** In several Nolimit City games, wilds drop to the bottom of their reel and stay until they are part of a win.
- **Sticky multiplier wilds.** In Thunderkick's [The Wildos](/slots/thunderkick/the-wildos), multiplier wilds stick in the bonus and keep boosting every win they join.
- **Growing sticky wilds.** In [Circle of Life](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/circle-of-life), a winning tree wild sticks and grows one row taller on each respin.

## Respin features

A **respin** is a free re-spin of some or all reels, triggered by something in the base game. Common patterns:

- **Win respins** – winning symbols stay and everything else respins. [Tomb of Nefertiti](/slots/nolimit-city/tomb-of-nefertiti) awards a respin on every win, with wilds held in place throughout.
- **Wild respins** – a new wild triggers a respin with all wilds held. [Ultimate Slot of America](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/ultimate-slot-of-america) keeps respinning as long as new Liberty Gems land.
- **Cluster respins** – [The Wildwood Curse](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/the-wildwood-curse) rewards wilds landing in a 2x2 square with special "cursed" clusters.

## Walking wilds

**Walking wilds** move one position each spin – usually one reel to the left – and pay again wherever they land. A wild that lands on the far right reel can pay up to five times before walking off the grid. See all of them in our [moving and walking wilds list](/best/moving-wilds-slots).

## Why these features are popular

Sticky and walking wilds create visible momentum: you can see what the next spin is building on. That makes them great for demo play and for learning a game. Volatility varies widely, though – a sticky multiplier wild in a high-volatility bonus can swing results far more than a simple win respin.
