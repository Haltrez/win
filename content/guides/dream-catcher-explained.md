**Dream Catcher** is the original Evolution money wheel, launched in 2017 – the game that Crazy Time, Monopoly Live and Funky Time grew out of. A live host spins a large vertical wheel; you bet on the number it stops on.

It's a live broadcast with **no free demo** (the RNG version, *First Person Dream Catcher*, sometimes has one). For free play, every slot on Scatter Drop has a [demo](/slots).

## The wheel

54 segments: the numbers **1, 2, 5, 10, 20 and 40**, plus two multiplier segments, **2x** and **7x**. The more segments a number has, the more often it hits and the less it pays:

| Number | Pays |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1:1 |
| 2 | 2:1 |
| 5 | 5:1 |
| 10 | 10:1 |
| 20 | 20:1 |
| 40 | 40:1 |

## Multiplier segments

When the wheel lands on **2x** or **7x**, no bets are settled. The host spins again, and the next number result is multiplied. If it lands on a multiplier again, the multipliers multiply together (2x then 7x gives 14x), and so on until a number comes up. That's how a 40 bet can pay far more than 40:1.

## RTP

Independent sources report **96.58%** for Dream Catcher, with the number bets slightly different from one another. It's in line with a typical slot and a bit below roulette.

## Why play it

It's the simplest live game show: one bet type, one spin, instant result. Good if you want a live host and a social feel without learning bonus rounds. If you want more features, [Crazy Time](/guides/crazy-time-explained) and [Monopoly Live](/guides/monopoly-live-explained) are built on the same wheel with bonus games added.
