A **sweepstakes casino** is a free-to-play social casino for US players. You play slot-style games with virtual coins, **no purchase is ever necessary**, and one of the two coin types – Sweeps Coins – can be redeemed for real prizes. That model is why sweepstakes casinos operate in most US states where regular online casinos don't.

## The two coins

Every sweepstakes casino uses two virtual currencies:

- **Gold Coins (GC)** – for fun only. You get them free when you sign up and from daily rewards, and you can buy more in coin packages. Gold Coins have **no cash value** and can never be redeemed.
- **Sweeps Coins (SC)** – the sweepstakes entries. You **can't buy them**. They come free: as a sign-up gift, from daily logins and mini-games, through a free mail-in request (often called AMOE – "alternative method of entry"), or as a free extra when you buy a Gold Coin package. Sweeps Coins won in play can be redeemed for prizes.

You switch between the two modes in the game lobby. In Gold Coin mode you're playing purely for fun; in Sweeps Coin mode you're entering the sweepstakes.

## Why it's legal in most states

US sweepstakes law allows prize promotions as long as **no purchase is necessary to enter** and there's a free way to take part. Sweepstakes casinos follow that rule: you buy Gold Coins (which have no cash value), Sweeps Coins are always free, and anyone can get them without paying through the daily rewards or the mail-in route.

Some states have restricted the model anyway, so availability depends on where you live – see our guide to [sweepstakes casino states](/guides/sweepstakes-casino-states).

## How redeeming prizes works

1. **Play with Sweeps Coins.** Most sites require Sweeps Coins to be played through once before they become redeemable.
2. **Reach the minimum.** Each site sets a minimum number of redeemable Sweeps Coins for a prize redemption.
3. **Verify your identity.** Before the first redemption you'll upload ID and sometimes proof of address. Your location is checked too.
4. **Choose how to receive it** – the methods (bank transfer, gift cards and others) vary by site.

Read each site's Sweeps Rules for the exact minimums and processing times.
