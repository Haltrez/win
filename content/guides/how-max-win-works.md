A slot's **max win** is the largest amount a single paid spin (including any bonus it triggers) can pay, expressed as a multiple of your stake. If a game has a **5,000x** max win and you bet €0.50, the most it can pay on one round is €2,500.

## Win caps

Most modern slots enforce the max win as a hard **cap**. When the running total of a spin or bonus reaches the cap, the round ends and you're paid exactly the cap. Casinos may also have their own maximum payout limits that are lower than the game's cap – check the operator's terms.

## Why bigger caps mean more volatility

RTP is fixed. If a game sets aside a meaningful share of its return for extremely rare 100,000x outcomes, that return has to come from somewhere – usually from fewer mid-sized wins. That's why games like [Tombstone RIP](/slots/nolimit-city/tombstone-rip) feel so punishing in normal play.

## Max win isn't a quality score

A higher max win doesn't make a slot better or give you better odds. We never increase a review score because of a large cap. Choose a slot for its gameplay and a volatility level you're comfortable with. See [volatility explained](/guides/slot-volatility-explained).

## Max wins across studios

The max win you'll see depends heavily on the studio. In our database the median published max win is about **5,000x** for BGaming and Pragmatic Play, **10,000x** for Hacksaw Gaming and **20,000x** for Nolimit City. Nolimit's [Tombstone Slaughter](/slots/nolimit-city/tombstone-slaughter) advertises 500,000x – the highest we list – and [San Quentin 2: Death Row](/slots/nolimit-city/san-quentin-2-death-row) 200,000x. Caps have also grown over time; our [max-win inflation study](/guides/slot-max-win-inflation) tracks how far, year by year.

## How rare is a max win?

Studios rarely publish the odds, but Nolimit City does for some games: its data puts the probability of reaching the max win in the tens of millions of spins for high-cap titles. In other words, the max win is a theoretical ceiling that almost no player ever sees. It's also why games with huge caps tend to have low [hit frequencies](/guides/hit-frequency-explained) and long dry spells.

## Capped rounds

When a round reaches the max win, the game usually ends the feature immediately and pays the capped amount, even if more spins remain. Some games state this explicitly, for example Nolimit City's [Space Donkey](/slots/nolimit-city/space-donkey).
