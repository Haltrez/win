In a **cluster pays** slot, you win when a group of matching symbols (usually five or more) touch each other horizontally or vertically anywhere on the grid. There are no paylines.

## Cluster vs pay anywhere

- **Cluster pays:** symbols must be connected. Example: [Reactoonz](/slots/play-n-go/reactoonz).
- **Pay anywhere / scatter pays:** symbols just need to appear in sufficient number anywhere. Example: [Sweet Bonanza](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza).

## Typical features

Cluster slots usually use cascades and add multiplier mechanics tied to grid positions – such as the doubling spots in [Sugar Rush](/slots/pragmatic-play/sugar-rush) – or moving multiplier wilds like the jars in [Jammin' Jars](/slots/push-gaming/jammin-jars).

See the full [cluster pays slots list](/best/cluster-pays-slots).

## How a cluster win is counted

A cluster pays slot checks the whole grid after every spin. Any group of matching symbols connected **horizontally or vertically** – diagonals usually don't count – forms a cluster. The payout depends on the symbol and the cluster size, and the paytable lists separate values for sizes like 5, 6–7, 8–9, 10–11 and 12+.

Bigger grids need bigger clusters. Thunderkick's 7x7 games such as [Hot Potato](/slots/thunderkick/hot-potato) and the 8x8 [Bjorn](/slots/thunderkick/bjorn) need five or more, while Nolimit City's 4x4 [DJ Psycho](/slots/nolimit-city/dj-psycho) pays from three.

## Cluster grids come in many shapes

- **Square grids** – the classic 7x7 or 8x8 board.
- **Hexagonal grids** – Thunderkick's [Greedy Pumpking](/slots/thunderkick/greedy-pumpking) and [Shark Park](/slots/thunderkick/shark-park) use a honeycomb, so each position has six neighbours instead of four.
- **Tiny grids** – Hacksaw's [Blocks](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/blocks) plays clusters on just nine cubes and lets you choose the minimum cluster size through a risk setting.

## Multipliers on cluster grids

Because clusters land in the same areas again and again, many studios attach multipliers to **grid positions** rather than symbols. A position that is part of a win gets a multiplier that grows each time it wins again. Nolimit City's [San Quentin Manhunt](/slots/nolimit-city/san-quentin-manhunt) gives every position its own Multiplier Block, and Thunderkick's multiplier-grid games work the same way.

## Is cluster pays good for beginners?

Cluster games are easy to *watch* but often high volatility, because the big wins depend on long cascade chains and growing multipliers.
