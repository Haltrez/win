In a **cluster pays** slot, you win when a group of matching symbols (usually five or more) touch each other horizontally or vertically anywhere on the grid. There are no paylines.

## Cluster vs pay anywhere

- **Cluster pays:** symbols must be connected. Example: [Reactoonz](/slots/play-n-go/reactoonz).
- **Pay anywhere / scatter pays:** symbols just need to appear in sufficient number anywhere. Example: [Sweet Bonanza](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza).

## Typical features

Cluster slots usually use cascades and add multiplier mechanics tied to grid positions – such as the doubling spots in [Sugar Rush](/slots/pragmatic-play/sugar-rush) – or moving multiplier wilds like the jars in [Jammin' Jars](/slots/push-gaming/jammin-jars).
