Every online slot is driven by a **random number generator (RNG)** – software that produces a new, unpredictable result for every spin. In regulated markets the RNG and the game maths are certified by independent test labs.

## Each spin is independent

The RNG has no memory. A slot isn't "hot", "cold" or "due" – the outcome of your next spin is not affected by previous spins, the time of day or how long you've been playing.

## The basics

- **Reels and rows:** the grid, e.g. 5x3 (five reels, three rows).
- **Paylines / ways:** the patterns that count as a win. Some games use clusters or pay anywhere instead.
- **Paytable:** what each symbol combination pays, shown as a multiple of your stake.
- **Wild:** substitutes for other symbols.
- **Scatter:** usually triggers the bonus regardless of position.
- **Bonus round:** typically free spins with extra features such as multipliers or sticky wilds.

## Three numbers to check

1. [RTP](/guides/rtp-explained) – how expensive the game is long-term.
2. [Volatility](/guides/slot-volatility-explained) – how swingy it is.
