Every online slot is driven by a **random number generator (RNG)** – software that produces a new, unpredictable result for every spin. In regulated markets the RNG and the game maths are certified by independent test labs.

## Each spin is independent

The RNG has no memory. A slot isn't "hot", "cold" or "due" – the outcome of your next spin is not affected by previous spins, the time of day or how long you've been playing.

## The basics

- **Reels and rows:** the grid, e.g. 5x3 (five reels, three rows).
- **Paylines / ways:** the patterns that count as a win. Some games use clusters or pay anywhere instead.
- **Paytable:** what each symbol combination pays, shown as a multiple of your stake.
- **Wild:** substitutes for other symbols.
- **Scatter:** usually triggers the bonus regardless of position.
- **Bonus round:** typically free spins with extra features such as multipliers or sticky wilds.

## Features: where the excitement lives

Base-game spins usually return a modest share of a slot's RTP. Most of the potential sits in features:

- **Free spins** with multipliers, expanding symbols or sticky wilds – see [free spins explained](/guides/free-spins-explained).
- **Hold and win** respin rounds with coins and jackpots – see [hold and win explained](/guides/hold-and-win-explained).
- **Tumbling reels** that let one spin pay several times – see [tumbling reels explained](/guides/tumbling-reels-explained).
- **Studio mechanics** such as Nolimit City's [xMechanics](/guides/nolimit-city-x-mechanics-explained) or Hacksaw's [DuelReels](/guides/duelreels-explained).

## Myths worth ignoring

- **"A slot is due to pay."** Each spin is independent. Past results have no effect on the next spin.
- **"Stopping the reels changes the result."** The outcome is decided the moment you press spin.
- **"Demo mode is rigged to pay more."** Official demos use the same game maths; they are simply short, random samples.

The best way to learn a slot is to read its review and play the [official demo](/guides/how-to-play-slot-demos) before you ever stake real money.
