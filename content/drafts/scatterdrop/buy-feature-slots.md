A buy feature slot lets you skip the wait. Instead of spinning until the bonus lands, you pay a fixed price, usually a multiple of your stake, and the bonus starts straight away. It's one of the most popular features in modern slots, and one of the easiest ways to burn through a budget. This guide shows real prices from official game data, what a buy actually changes, and which free demos let you test it at no cost.

## TL;DR

- A feature buy costs a fixed multiple of your stake, often around 80x to 100x, and much more for premium tiers.
- The price is set so the average bonus is worth roughly the price, minus the house edge. Buying doesn't improve your odds.
- What it changes is speed and swing: many bought bonuses return less than they cost.
- Feature buys are banned in Great Britain and restricted in some other markets.

## How a feature buy works

1. **Pick the buy button** and see the price shown as a multiple of your stake.
2. **Pay once.** At a 1.00 stake, a 100x buy costs 100.00.
3. **The bonus starts** exactly as if it had triggered naturally, usually free spins.
4. **The result pays** whatever the bonus produces, from a few times your stake to the game's max win.

Some games sell more than one product. Hacksaw games such as [Wanted Dead or a Wild](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/wanted-dead-or-a-wild) let you buy different bonus tiers, and Nolimit City adds options like the Toast Bet in [Folsom Prison](/slots/nolimit-city/folsom-prison): a 20% extra cost per spin for an assured scatter on reel 2.

## How to read the numbers

- **RTP** is the long-run share of stakes paid back. A buy is priced so its own RTP is close to the game's. Some games list a separate buy RTP in the paytable.
- **Volatility** matters even more with buys: one bought bonus can return 5x or 500x. High-volatility games make that spread wider.
- **Max win** is the ceiling of one round, like Tombstone RIP's 300,000x. It's the rarest result in the game, not something a buy makes likely.

## Playing responsibly

At a 1.00 stake, ten 100x buys cost 1,000.00 in a few minutes. If you use buys at all, budget them as a number of buys, not spins, and stop when that number is used.
