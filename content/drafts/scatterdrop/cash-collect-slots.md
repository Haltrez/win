Cash collect slots have a simple, satisfying hook: money symbols land with values on them, and a collector sweeps them all up. The fishing games made it famous, with the fisherman reeling in every fish on screen, but the idea now appears in everything from pandas to tanks. This guide explains how collectors work, the main variations, and which free demos show the mechanic best.

## TL;DR

- Money symbols carry cash values, usually multiples of your bet. A collector symbol adds them up and pays the total.
- Most of the action happens in free spins, where collectors appear more often or stay for the whole round.
- Multipliers on the collector, or collectors that stay on the grid, are where the big wins come from.

## How cash collect works

1. **Money symbols land** with values on them.
2. **A collector lands** on the same spin, often a wild or a special character.
3. **The collector adds up** every money value on screen and pays the total, sometimes with a multiplier.

In [Big Bass Bonanza](/slots/pragmatic-play/big-bass-bonanza), fish carry random cash values. In free spins, each fisherman wild collects every fish value on screen, and every fourth wild retriggers spins and raises the collect multiplier (2x, 3x, 10x).

## The main variations

- **Multiplier trails.** Collecting enough collectors raises a multiplier, as in Big Bass Bonanza.
- **Collectors that stay.** In Wazdan's [Mighty Wild: Gorilla](/slots/wazdan/mighty-wild-gorilla), Collector symbols stay for up to 10 spins, collecting Cash and Jackpot values below them. In [Fisherman's Luck](/slots/wazdan/fishermans-luck), Gainer symbols do the same, and in Free Spins they stay for the whole round.
- **Mystery reveals.** In [Big Bamboo](/slots/push-gaming/big-bamboo), mystery bamboo reveals coins, multipliers, collectors or symbols.
- **Reel-based collecting.** In [Wild Wild Pearls](/slots/pragmatic-play/wild-wild-pearls), wilds on reels 1–2 collect money symbols on reels 3–6, and filling a reel with money applies its multiplier.
- **Character collecting.** In Nolimit City's [Tanked](/slots/nolimit-city/tanked), characters collect symbols of their own colour with xLoot.
- **Square-based collecting.** Hacksaw's [Le Bandit](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/le-bandit) turns winning positions golden, and Rainbow symbols reveal coins, clovers and pots on them.
