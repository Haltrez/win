The scatter is the symbol that starts the fun. Unlike regular symbols, it counts wherever it lands, and landing enough of them usually triggers the bonus. Some games go further and let every symbol pay like a scatter, with no paylines at all. This guide explains how scatters work, the difference between scatter triggers and scatter pays, Pragmatic Play's newer Super Scatters, and free demos where you can see each one.

## TL;DR

- A scatter counts anywhere on the reels, not just on a payline.
- Three or four scatters usually trigger free spins. Some games award more spins for more scatters.
- Scatter pays ("pay anywhere") means every symbol works this way: 8 or more matching symbols anywhere win.
- Super Scatters pay an instant prize on top of triggering the bonus.
- Ante bets make scatters land more often, at a higher cost per spin.

## How scatters work

1. **They land anywhere.** Position doesn't matter. Only the count does.
2. **Enough of them trigger the bonus.** In [Sweet Bonanza](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza), 4+ lollipop scatters award 10 free spins.
3. **Some pay on their own.** Many games pay a small prize for the scatters themselves.
4. **Some double as wilds.** In [Book of Dead](/slots/play-n-go/book-of-dead), the book is both the wild and the scatter.

Ante bets target scatters directly. In Sweet Bonanza, the ante bet raises the stake by 25% to increase scatter frequency. Read more in [ante bet explained](/guides/ante-bet-explained).

## Scatter pays: when every symbol is a scatter

Some games drop paylines entirely. In a "pay anywhere" or "scatter pays" game, a set number of matching symbols anywhere on the grid wins:

- [Sweet Bonanza](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza) and [Starlight Princess](/slots/pragmatic-play/starlight-princess): 8 or more matching symbols anywhere on a 6x5 grid, with tumbles after each win.
- Nolimit City's [Duck Hunters](/slots/nolimit-city/duck-hunters): 8 or more matching symbols anywhere, with xWays position multipliers of 2x, 4x or 8x.
- Thunderkick's [Xterminate](/slots/thunderkick/xterminate): scatter pays plus an Xterminate multiplier and charged wilds.

These games usually pair scatter pays with cascades and multipliers, so most of their value sits in long chains during the bonus.

## Super Scatters

Pragmatic Play's Super Scatter editions add a special scatter that pays an instant prize when it helps trigger the bonus. In [Gates of Olympus Super Scatter](/slots/pragmatic-play/gates-of-olympus-super-scatter), one to four Super Scatters in the trigger pay 100x, 500x, 5,000x or the 50,000x maximum. [Sweet Bonanza Super Scatter](/slots/pragmatic-play/sweet-bonanza-super-scatter) works the same way. Read [Super Scatter slots explained](/guides/super-scatter-slots-explained) for every edition.
