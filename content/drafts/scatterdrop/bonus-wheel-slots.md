Wheel features turn the bonus into a little game show: the wheel spins, slows down and lands on a prize. Some wheels award multipliers, others free spins, jackpots or a shot at the max win. This guide explains the main types of bonus wheel, shows real examples from official game data, and lists free demos where you can watch them spin before playing for real.

## TL;DR

- A bonus wheel decides part of a win: a multiplier, extra spins, a feature or a jackpot.
- The result is random and part of the game's RTP. The animation adds suspense but doesn't change the odds.
- Wheels can be frequent and small, as in BGaming's Arrow, or rare and huge, as in Hacksaw's SixSixSix.
- Several wheels in a row can multiply each other, which is where very high max wins come from.
- Try wheel slots in demo first to see how often the wheel appears and what it usually pays.

## Types of bonus wheel

**Feature wheels** pick which bonus you get. [Blitz Super Wheel](/slots/pragmatic-play/blitz-super-wheel) randomly awards one of seven features on any spin, including free spins and a Pot Prize from five jackpots up to 10,000x.

**Multiplier wheels** add or multiply values. Hacksaw's [SixSixSix](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/sixsixsix) uses Wicked Wheels that award adding or multiplying values, free spins or the max win, and the left-to-right order of the results decides the final multiplier. [Pray for Three](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/pray-for-three) has a Wheel of Sin with up to three sections, with Prayer Hands deciding how many activate.

**Prize-ladder wheels** aim at fixed amounts. In [Power of Ten](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/power-of-ten), Power Wheels award 10x, 100x, 1,000x or the max win, adding together, and five wheels on one row raise the possible values on every wheel.

**Bonus-trigger wheels** start the bonus itself. In [Arrow](/slots/bgaming/arrow), scatters at the arrow's tip trigger the Wheel of Fortune, about once every 20 spins on average.

**Grid-changing wheels** alter the reels. In [Fortuna Trueways](/slots/bgaming/fortuna-trueways), the Wheel of Fortune can split cells to add more ways.

## How to read the numbers

- **RTP:** wheel outcomes are already part of it. A colourful wheel doesn't mean a better return.
- **Volatility:** frequent, small wheels (Arrow) make for a steadier game. Wheels that multiply each other (SixSixSix) make for a very swingy one.
- **Max win:** on wheel games it's usually reached by stacking several top results, the least likely outcome.
