Wild symbols are the oldest special symbol in slots: they stand in for other symbols to complete a win. Modern games give them extra jobs. They stick, expand, walk across the reels, multiply wins or nudge into place. This guide explains every common type of wild, with real examples from official game data and free demos where you can see each one in action.

## TL;DR

- A wild substitutes for other symbols, usually all except scatters and bonus symbols.
- The main types are sticky, expanding, walking, multiplier, nudging and spreading wilds.
- Multiplier wilds that stick or combine are where the biggest wins come from, and the biggest swings.
- On Scatter Drop, 94 slots have sticky wilds, 101 expanding wilds and 29 walking wilds.
- Try each type in demo to see which style you actually enjoy.

## Types of wild symbol

**Multiplier wilds** multiply any win they're part of. In [Wild West Gold](/slots/pragmatic-play/wild-west-gold), wilds carry 2x, 3x or 5x, and several in the same win are added together.

**Sticky wilds** stay in place. In Wild West Gold's free spins, every multiplier wild stays where it lands for the rest of the round. In [Wildies](/slots/pragmatic-play/wildies), wilds stay while respins continue until no new wilds land, and up to five can carry 10x.

**Expanding wilds** grow to fill a reel. In Hacksaw's [Hot Ross](/slots/hacksaw-gaming/hot-ross), Ro$$ symbols expand into full reels, and expanding through a wild adds a multiplier of up to 200x.

**Expanding symbols** are a close cousin. In [Book of Dead](/slots/play-n-go/book-of-dead), a random symbol chosen before free spins expands to fill reels and pays on any position.

**Walking wilds** move across the reels between spins. In Thunderkick's [Riders of the Storm](/slots/thunderkick/riders-of-the-storm), walking wilds move during the bonus.

**Nudging wilds** slide into view and gain value as they do. In Nolimit City's [Brute Force](/slots/nolimit-city/brute-force), three types of stacked xNudge Wilds carry random multipliers, and a meter adds up all the multipliers on winning symbols.

**Shaped and spreading wilds** cover patterns. In [Possessed](/slots/nolimit-city/possessed), a wild at the joint turns a whole cross wild at x2. In [Infectious 5 xWays](/slots/nolimit-city/infectious-5-xways), Infectious xWays reveal symbol stacks and spread them across the reels.

## How to read the numbers

- **RTP:** wilds are part of the published figure. More wilds on screen doesn't mean a better return.
- **Volatility:** simple substitute wilds keep a game steady. Multiplier, sticky and nudging wilds concentrate value into rare big moments, which is why Nolimit City's wild-heavy games are rated very high.
- **Max win:** games where wilds stack or multiply each other have the highest caps in this list.
