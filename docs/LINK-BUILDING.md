# Backlink & authority plan

Principles: editorial and niche-relevant links only, natural velocity, no PBNs, no paid sitewide links, no link exchanges at scale. Gambling is a YMYL niche; one manual action can erase years of work.

## Targets

| Phase | Timeframe | Referring domains (cumulative) | Monthly new RDs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | Pre-launch → month 3 | 50–100 | 15–30 |
| Growth | Months 4–12 | 200–400 | 20–35 |
| Authority | Months 13–36 | 800–1,500+ | 30–50 (only if brand demand grows) |

Healthy mix target at month 12: ~40% editorial (PR/data studies, journalist citations), ~25% niche guest/contributor content, ~15% resource & directory pages (relevant, curated), ~10% partnerships (providers, events, communities), ~10% brand/nav/profile links. Anchor text: >60% branded/URL, <10% exact-match money terms.

## Foundation (months 0–3, 50–100 RDs)

1. **Brand & profile set (15–25):** social profiles, Crunchbase, relevant industry directories (curated, not mass), affiliate program listings, iGaming affiliate conference attendee pages.
2. **Responsible-gambling resource pages (5–10):** offer genuinely useful RG guides (self-exclusion by country) to support organisations' and university resource pages. Never pitch casino content to these.
3. **Niche guest/contributor posts (20–40):** iGaming news sites, affiliate industry blogs, slot community sites. Topics: data-led pieces (RTP variance, max-win inflation), not "best casinos" articles. Max ~2 links per post; mostly branded anchors.
4. **Provider relationships (5–10):** studios link to sites that review their games; request inclusion on "press/media" or "where to play our demos" pages as you cover their catalogue.
5. **Broken link building (10–20):** find dead links on gambling help/education pages and on iGaming sites to defunct slot databases; offer the equivalent page.

## Growth (months 4–12, → 200–400 RDs)

- **Data studies as link magnets** (Cluster G in the content plan): "Average RTP of 3,000 slots", "Max-win inflation 2015–2026", "Reduced-RTP versions study". One major study per quarter, pitched to iGaming press + mainstream data/tech journalists. Expect 15–60 RDs per successful study.
- **Embeddable assets:** RTP lookup widget / volatility chart with attribution link.
- **Digital PR reactive:** comment on regulation news (UKGC stake limits, bonus-buy bans) with expert quotes (Qwoted, HARO alternatives, journalist Twitter/X/Bluesky).
- **Unlinked brand mentions:** monitor and request links.
- **Community:** contribute to slot forums/Reddit/Discord honestly (no link drops); sponsor small community tournaments where legal.
- **Podcasts & interviews:** affiliate/iGaming podcasts.

## Authority (13–36 months)

- Annual "State of Slots" report.
- Conference talks (iGB Affiliate, SiGMA, ICE) → event-site links.
- Original tools (slot RTP checker, bankroll simulator) that earn natural links.
- Scale only if brand searches and direct traffic grow; link velocity should follow content and brand growth.

## Process & tracking

- Weekly: prospect list (Ahrefs/Semrush link intersect against Bigwinboard-style competitors), outreach sequences (personalised), follow-ups.
- Track in a sheet/CRM: domain, DR/traffic, topical relevance (0–3), link type, anchor, target URL, date, cost (if any sponsored – must be `rel="sponsored"`).
- Quality gate per link: real organic traffic, gambling-relevant or editorial, not a "write for us" farm, outbound link ratio sane, no casino-spam footprint.
- Monthly: disavow review (only for clear toxic attacks), anchor distribution check, velocity check (no spikes > 2× the 3-month average).
- Internal link targets for acquired links: guides & data studies first (they pass equity to category pages via internal links), then category pages; rarely slot pages directly.
